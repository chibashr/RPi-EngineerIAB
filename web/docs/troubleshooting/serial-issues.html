Serial issues

Resolve device detection and Serial console session problems.

Device not detected

Symptom: No devices listed in Advanced → Serial console.

Common causes: Adapter not connected or unsupported chipset.

Resolution: Reconnect the adapter and refresh the page.

Permission denied

Symptom: Cannot open device or session fails to start.

Common causes: Serial device permissions missing.

Resolution: Reboot or re-run the installer to apply permissions.

Garbled output

Symptom: Output is unreadable.

Common causes: Incorrect baud rate or parity.

Resolution: Update device settings to match the target device.
